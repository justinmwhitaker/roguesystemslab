# Project Triage Prospecting Run

Run date: 2026-06-11

## Product

**Project Triage** is a fast async diagnostic for stalled projects, messy delivery, scope creep, unclear ownership, missed deadlines, stakeholder misalignment, and execution bottlenecks.

## ICP and scoring model

Target buyers: founders, operators, project managers, program managers, delivery leads, agency owners, consultants, COOs, and small business owners.

Buying signals used in this run:

- Public mention of scope creep, over-servicing, budget overruns, or margin leakage.
- Public mention of missed deadlines, delivery delays, slow handoffs, messy coordination, unclear ownership, or stakeholder misalignment.
- Public evidence that the company delivers client work, implementation work, web/software/product projects, marketing programs, or cross-functional operations where project triage is relevant.
- Public evidence of growth, client count, multi-service delivery, or leadership role that suggests budget and authority.

Scoring is out of 25 points:

- Buyer fit: 1-5
- Signal strength: 1-5
- Urgency/timing: 1-5
- Ability to buy / authority: 1-5
- Personalization quality from public evidence: 1-5

**Use constraints:** The names below are prospects, not verified hand-raisers. Do not imply they asked for help. Use the public source as a personalization hook and frame outreach as a low-pressure diagnostic.

## Recommended next steps

1. Start with the top five prospects because their public signals are the most direct and personalized.
2. Use the source-specific hook in the first sentence; avoid generic “scope creep is hard” language.
3. Offer a diagnostic template first, not a meeting.
4. Do not pitch software. Pitch clarity: bottleneck map, owner map, tradeoff list, and next-action sequence.
5. Track replies by pain category: scope drift, ownership gaps, approval bottlenecks, stakeholder misalignment, capacity constraints, or missed deadlines.
